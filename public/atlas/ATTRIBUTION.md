# Anatomy model attribution

`head-neck.glb` is a simplified subset (head, neck, skull, cervical vertebrae, head and neck muscles) adapted from:

- BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/) and CC BY 4.0 (https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html).
- Z-Anatomy by Gauthier Kervyn, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), for the facial muscles.
- Mesh selection and packaging via BodyExplorer by Johan Bellander (MIT): https://github.com/JohanBellander/BodyExplorer

Adaptations: axes converted (millimetres, Z-up to 100 mm units, Y-up), meshes welded, decimated with meshoptimizer, normals recomputed, selection limited to the head and neck.
